    Welcome to the GurrLab¶

    Oregon State University · Hatfield Marine Science Center · Newport, Oregon

    This guide is your starting point for life in the GurrLab. It covers everything from lab culture and expectations to computing workflows, physical spaces, and role-specific onboarding paths.

    This guide is iterative and input from colleagues and lab members is expected to strengthen the lab mission. Feedback is welcome at samuel.gurr@oregonstate.edu.
